Your units

    How it works

    1. Create a unit. It gets its own link, and its own MCP URL for Claude.
    2. Let Claude build it. Add the MCP URL to Claude as a connector, share your floor plan and photos, and Claude builds the rooms, doors, windows, kitchen and baths to scale.
    3. Furnish it and share it. Place furniture yourself or ask Claude, walk through it, measure anything, and send a link anyone can open or make their own version of.

    Behind this panel is the example unit, a 2 bed, 2 bath apartment. Look around: drag to orbit, or switch to Plan or Walk.

            Share

            Share this page from the Share menu at the top of the Artifact in Claude. What people can do depends on their access:

            • Editors furnish it. Everyone sees their changes.
            • Contributors make their own version and suggest it.
            • Viewers and people with a public link look around.
            • Signed-out visitors see the layout as Claude last published it.
