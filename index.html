    How it was measured

    • Walls and openings are traced from the leasing plan at 25.86 px per foot, which reproduces all three printed room sizes within 2".
    • Ceiling height 9'0" comes from solving where each photo was taken, using wall corners and floor and ceiling lines that the plan locates.
    • Living room window is a 6'11" opening centered on the wall, measured from the photo. The plan symbol draws it about 9'. Sills sit about 23" up and heads line up with the 8' doors.
    • Kitchen follows the photo: fridge, base cabinet, and range along the back wall; sink and dishwasher on the side wall facing the dining area.
    • Primary bath follows the photos (walk-in shower with a glass panel, vanity and toilet side by side). The plan shows a corner shower.
    • Bath 2 and Bedroom 2 weren't photographed, so they follow the plan with matching finishes.
